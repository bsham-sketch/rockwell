How we build it / 08 stations + handover

How a site requirement becomes a proven crane.

We size each crane to your building, load and duty. Follow how those requirements become a fitted machine, proof-loaded to 125% of its rated capacity before handover.

Inside the works ↗
01

Station 01 / 08

Drawing office

The duty class comes first. Building, hook approach and wheel loads are worked to IS 807 and IS 3177 and issued as a GA drawing before fabrication.

02

Station 02 / 08

Plate marking & profile cutting

Plates are nested, marked and profile cut for webs, flanges, diaphragms and end carriage plates.

03

Station 03 / 08

Box girder fit-up

Webs, flanges and internal diaphragms are set on the jig, squared and tacked into a closed box with the design camber.

04

Station 04 / 08

Submerged arc welding

Long web-to-flange seams run under flux on the welding manipulator, with full penetration welds from both sides.

05

Station 05 / 08

Camber check & machining

The welded girder is checked for camber and sweep; bores, wheel seats and drum shaft housings are machined in line.

06

Station 06 / 08

End carriages & long travel

Wheels, axles and bearings are assembled; long travel drives are mounted and the bridge is set on its runway rails.

07

Station 07 / 08

Crab, drum & reeving

The hoist frame carries the rope drum, gearbox, brake motor and sheave blocks; the hook block is reeved and run through its lift.

08

Station 08 / 08

Trial assembly & proof load

The complete machine is assembled and loaded to 125% of safe working load; deflection, brakes, limits and travel are proved.

09

09 / Inspection & handover

Proved, inspected, ready for site.

Each crane is proof loaded to 125% of safe working load. Third-party inspection is available from RITES, EIL, MECON or DNV; test certificates go with the machine.

Before it leaves the works

Every machine is load tested.

Every machine is load tested at 25% overload. Third party inspection by RITES, EIL, MECON or DNV is available on request, with test certificates.
