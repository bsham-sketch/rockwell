Construction

Details from the works.

  • Safety Guaranteed by a braking valve acting together with the lowering valve. The robust hydraulic lifting unit has a pressure relief valve to prevent overloading.
  • Wheels All rear and steering wheels are of graded cast iron running on ball bearings.
  • Mast Stackers with 1.5 m lifting height and above are supported with a telescopic mast. Forks are lifted with tested wire rope to IS 2266, construction 6 × 37 FMC.
  • Guarantee 12 months from the date of billing against any manufacturing defect.
  • Design change Design may be changed for better technical feasibility without prior notice.
