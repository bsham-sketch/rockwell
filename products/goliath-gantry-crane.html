Goliath / Gantry Crane

Outdoor gantries for hydro-electric projects, shipping yards and mines.

Gantry cranes of different types are used in hydro-electric projects, shipping yards and mines where materials are handled in bulk. Gantry cranes without leg lifting arrangements are also manufactured.

This type of gantry crane is most often used in hydro-electric projects for lifting stop log gates. The cranes are built so that they suit operation outdoors and in all weather conditions.

For NHDC we designed, manufactured, supplied and commissioned a 180 tonne SWL gantry crane with an out-of-log lifting facility.

Typical applications

Hydro-electric projectsBarrages and damsShipping yardsMines and stockyards
  • Rail mounted and rubber tyred versions
  • Out-of-log lifting facility available
