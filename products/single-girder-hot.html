Single Girder H.O.T. Crane

The lightest hand operated travelling crane in the range.

Single girder hand operated travelling cranes are used where utilisation is less frequent and the required speed is slow. The single girder construction keeps dead weight on the gantry low and simplifies the supporting structure.

Typical applications

Light workshopsGodownsService bays
  • Light dead weight on the building structure
  • Hand chain operation from floor level
  • Economical for low duty cycles
  • Low head room construction
