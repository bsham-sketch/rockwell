Electric Power Driven Winch

Heavy duty winches to IS 9507, in single or variable speed.

Rockwell electric power driven winch machines are designed as per IS 9507-1979 and are suitable for heavy duty applications. Winches are available in single or variable speeds as required; speeds other than those in the technical data can also be supplied. We generally manufacture winches with right hand operation, however the same can be supplied with left hand operation if desired.

Construction

Details from the works.

  • Base frame Fabricated from rolled steel sections and plates, properly ribbed, floored with surface plate for the working area, with lifting lugs and anchoring holes for foundation.
  • Reducer First stage reduction through a worm reduction gear box of reputed make; further reduction through open spur gears and pinions.
  • Rope drum Steel fabricated drum, duly machined, flanged at both ends, winding capacity 100 m of wire rope. Larger winding capacities supplied on request.
  • Wire rope 6 × 19 IWRC construction, best plough steel conforming to IS 2266, supplied at extra cost.
  • Motor High starting torque, class B insulation, 150 starts/hour, 415 V 50 Hz 3-phase A.C. induction, squirrel cage or slip ring, conforming to IS 325.
  • Control panel Operated by push button station or controller at 110 V fixed on the machine. Triple pole reversing contactors, H.R.C. fuses and pilot lamps housed in a dust and vermin proof sheet steel panel. A step down transformer is provided for operator safety.
  • Brake Electromagnetic or electro-hydraulic thruster, double shoe type, and/or hand operated brake as required.
  • Limit switch Rotary gear type limit switch to prevent over hoisting or over travelling, provided at extra cost.
