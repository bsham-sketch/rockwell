Gates & Hydro-Mechanical Equipment

Gates, stop logs, hoisting bridges and embedded parts for hydro and irrigation projects.

Our hydro-mechanical division supplies equipment for hydro-electric power projects and hydro-irrigation projects. This equipment includes special duty E.O.T. cranes, specially designed gantry cranes, stop log gates, radial gates, draft tube gates, intake gates, spillway gates and vertical gates. We also manufacture specially designed hoisting arrangements for lifting stop log gates and sluice gates.

Our well equipped manufacturing unit, with state of the art equipment and technically qualified manpower, ensures time bound and top quality products.
