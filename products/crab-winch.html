Construction

Details from the works.

  • Gears A multi-geared machine: driver gear, reduction gear, pinion gear and main load gear, mounted on two ball bearings. Fabricated from tested steel, machined, with teeth cut by hob.
  • Rope drum M.S. pipe welded at both ends with flanges.
  • Body plates M.S. tested plates conforming to IS 226-1975.
  • Brakes Two brakes for safety — a pawl and ratchet brake preventing reverse movement of the drum under load, and a shoe type brake with replaceable lining.
  • Speed Varied by shifting gears.
  • Operating handles Two handles supplied with each machine, designed to pull heavy loads with the least effort.
  • Test All machines are tested at 25% overload.
  • Guarantee 12 months from the date of delivery against any manufacturing defect, at factory basis.
