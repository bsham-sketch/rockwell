Construction

Details from the works.

  • Construction Strong and robust with the correct weight to capacity ratio.
  • Lowering lever Mounted on the steering handle for easy operation.
  • Hydraulics Leak proof system incorporating valves, cylinder, reservoir and overload safety device. The ram is hardened chromium plated.
  • Handle Spring pressure keeps the handle in the vertical position.
  • Wheels Grey iron casting wheels fitted with double bearings in each wheel for smooth movement.
  • Safety A pressure relief valve is fitted to prevent overloading.
  • Control Pump control from the steering handle gives high leverage for fast and easy lifting, and makes 180° side turning very smooth.
