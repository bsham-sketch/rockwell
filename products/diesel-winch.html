Construction

Details from the works.

  • Base frame Fabricated from rolled steel sections and plates, properly ribbed, floored with surface plate for the working area, with lifting lugs and anchoring holes for foundation.
  • Reducer First stage reduction through a worm reduction gear box of reputed make; further reduction through open spur gears and pinions.
  • Bearings Self aligning bearings of reputed make.
  • Rope drum Steel fabricated drum, duly machined, flanged at both ends.
  • Wire rope 6 × 19 IWRC construction, best plough steel conforming to IS 2266, supplied at extra cost.
  • Brakes Foot operated band brakes with ferrodo wire woven lining.
  • Clutch Mechanically operated expansion type hand operated clutch with wire woven lining, easily replaceable.
  • Drive Industrial diesel engine — Kirloskar or Perkins, at extra cost.
