Details from the works.

  • Frame Robust and strong, manufactured from rolled steel sections.
  • Steering Easy control by steering.
  • Hydraulics Leak proof hydraulic system fitted with a release valve, giving infinitely variable lowering speeds.
  • Wheels Front and rear wheels in graded grey iron casting, fitted with sealed ball bearings for smooth movement.
  • Hook Forged hook mounted on a thrust bearing for 360° swivelling.
  • Jib arm Adjustable to allow three different hook positions.
