Single Girder E.O.T. Crane

Moderate capacity workshop cranes for buildings where head room is tight.

These are moderate capacity workshop cranes manufactured for capacities up to 20 tonnes. This type of crane is normally operated from the floor through a pendant, and is also suitable where head room — bracket top to ceiling — is limited.

Typical applications

WorkshopsAssembly shopsStorage yardsMaintenance bays
  • Capacities from 1 t to 20 t
  • Spans from 5 m to 20 m
  • Floor operated through pendant control
  • Low head room configuration
