Construction

Details from the works.

  • Girder Heavy duty beam conforming to IS 226 of 1975.
  • Pipes Four male and four female E.R.W. pipes conforming to IS 1161 of 1963.
  • Wheels Four castor wheel assemblies, 360° swivelling, fitted with two ball bearings each for smooth and light movement. Each assembly has a locking device.
  • Winches Each side of the crane carries a winch machine with wire rope for smooth and easy erection.
  • Self erection Two laymen can erect the complete system with the help of the winch machines supplied with the crane.
  • Test Tested at 25% overload.
  • Warranty 12 months from the date of supply against any manufacturing defect, at factory basis.
