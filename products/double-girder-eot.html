Double Girder E.O.T. Crane

Designed and manufactured to the specific requirements of each customer and to site conditions.

Double girder E.O.T. cranes are designed and manufactured as per the specific requirements of customers and according to site conditions. Some of the applications of this type of crane are in power houses for handling power generating equipment and its parts, and as grabbing cranes for handling materials like cement, coal and sand.

General duty cranes serve workshops and manufacturing units handling raw materials through to finished goods. We also manufacture special duty cranes for handling liquid metal, where conditions are very hot and hazardous. Flame proof cranes are supplied according to the requirement of the site.

Typical applications

Power housesSteel plantsCement and coal handlingHeavy workshops
  • Built to IS 807 and IS 3177 of 1977
  • Capacities to 150 tonnes, spans to 20 m and beyond
  • Grab, magnet and ladle duty versions
  • Flame proof construction available
  • Cabin, pendant or radio remote control
