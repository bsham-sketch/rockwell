Electric Wire Rope Hoist

Modular monorail hoists built so every sub-assembly comes apart on its own.

These units lift and move materials in to-and-fro motion along a monorail or as the hoisting unit of a crane.

Rockwell hoists have a unique modular construction which makes them an ideal choice, since each sub assembly is individually accessible — reducing the time and cost involved in maintenance.

Typical applications

Monorail handlingCrane hoisting unitsGate lifting at damsWorkshops
  • Capacities from 250 kg to 30 tonnes
  • Modular sub-assemblies for fast maintenance
  • Monorail, double rail and fixed suspension
  • Gate lifting hoists at dams up to 125 tonnes
