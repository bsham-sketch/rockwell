Double Girder H.O.T. Crane

Hand operated travelling cranes for duties where crane use is infrequent.

These cranes are used where utilisation is less frequent and the required speed is slow. Travel, cross travel and hoisting are all hand operated through chains from floor level, which removes any dependence on a power supply at the crane.

Typical applications

Standby liftingSub-stationsPump housesInfrequent maintenance duty
  • Capacities to 50 tonnes
  • No power supply required at the crane
  • Geared trolley and chain pulley block
  • Suited to standby and infrequent duty
