Our own works

Every girder on every crane we have supplied was cut, welded, machined and assembled at Plot 14, Sector 16, HSIIDC Bahadurgarh — by our own people, on our own plant, under our own drawings.

That matters when something is non-standard. A barrage gantry with out-of-log lifting, a crane that has to fit a building that was never designed for one, a replacement girder for a machine somebody else built in 1994 — all of it is the same shop, the same welders and the same inspection.

Station by station

The eight stations a crane passes through.

Station 01 / 08
01 · Drawing office

General arrangement

  • Duty class M3 to M8 to IS 3177
  • Wheel loads issued for your gantry girder
  • Approval drawing before fabrication
02 · Cutting bay

Plate marking & profile cutting

  • Profile cutting to nested layouts
  • Edge preparation for full penetration welds
  • Material traceable to test certificate
03 · Fit-up bay

Box girder fit-up

  • Diaphragms pitched to the design
  • Squared and tacked on the jig
  • Camber set before welding
04 · Welding bay

Submerged arc welding

  • Submerged arc on the long seams
  • Welders qualified to the procedure
  • Welds checked before the girder moves on
05 · Machine shop

Camber check & machining

  • Camber and sweep verified after welding
  • End carriage bores machined in line
  • Wheel treads and flanges to profile
06 · Assembly bay

End carriages & long travel

  • Double flanged wheels on tapered bearings
  • LT drive with brake motor each end
  • Bridge squared on the runway
07 · Crab bay

Crab, drum & reeving

  • Grooved drum with rope anchor plates
  • Four or eight fall reeving to the duty
  • Limit switches set at both extremes
08 · Test bay

Trial assembly & proof load

  • Proof load at 125% of SWL
  • Deflection measured against the design
  • Test certificates issued with the crane

Inspection & testing

Somebody else gets to check our work.

From time to time our equipment is inspected and passed by third party agencies, at the customer's instruction or ours.

Measured, not assumed

Camber, sweep and deflection are measured against the design figure at the stages where they can still be corrected.

125% proof load

Every machine is loaded beyond its rating before it leaves, with the test certificates issued against it.

Qualified welders

Long seams run to procedure by welders who have been doing this work on this floor for years.

Twelve month guarantee

Against defect in material and workmanship, from the date of commissioning.
